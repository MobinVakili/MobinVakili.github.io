Mobin Vakili

Environmental Engineer & Researcher

Welcome to my portfolio.

I am an Environmental Engineer with a focus in sustainable groundwater management, contamination transport, and hydrogeological modeling. I earned my M.Sc. in Environmental Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic), ranked 3rd in my cohort. Prior to this, I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at Isfahan University of Technology.

During my master's studies, I worked on the experimental investigation and machine learning modeling of boron removal from groundwater using Permeable Reactive Barriers. I used artificial intelligence algorithms such as XGBoost, Random Forest, and ANFIS via Python and MATLAB to build predictive models for optimizing environmental remediation systems.

Beyond the laboratory, I have mentored over 90 students as a Teaching Assistant for Environmental Geotechnics. Additionally, my professional background includes working for the United Nations, where I wrote and translated environmental documents, and produced digital content.
